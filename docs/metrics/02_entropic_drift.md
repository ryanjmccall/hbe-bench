# Metric 2: Entropic Drift ($\Delta S$)
### The Immortality Test

> "A chatbot can fake being you for 5 minutes. Only a topological soliton can remain 'you' for 1,000 years."

Most benchmarks test for **Fidelity** (How much does the copy look like the original *right now*?). HBE-BENCH tests for **Permanence** (Will the copy dissolve into noise over time?). This is the filter that separates "Simulations" from "Souls."

#### 1. The Physics Problem: The Second Law
In any dynamical system, small numerical errors (quantization noise, floating-point rounding) accumulate over time.
* **In AI:** This manifests as "Hallucination" or mode collapse.
* **In WBE:** This manifests as **Dementia** and eventually **Space-Time Death.**
* **The Trap:** If the digital brain requires constant external error-correction (rebooting) to stay sane, it is not a "Self." It is a managed service.

#### 2. The Solution: Zero Entropy Production
We are looking for a substrate with $\frac{dS}{dt} \approx 0$. This requires the mind to function as a **Time Crystal** or a **Topological Soliton**—a structure that repeats and processes information without dissipating energy into heat (information loss). It must be **Thermodynamically Reversible**.

#### 3. The Metric Equation
We measure the divergence of the system's attractor landscape over $t$ subjective years.

$$
\text{Drift} = \frac{dS}{dt} = \lim_{\Delta t \to \infty} \frac{D_{KL}(P_{t} || P_{t+\Delta t})}{\Delta t}
$$

* **$P_t$**: The probability distribution of the mind's state space at time $t$.
* **$D_{KL}$**: The Kullback-Leibler divergence (measuring how much the "shape" of the mind has distorted).
