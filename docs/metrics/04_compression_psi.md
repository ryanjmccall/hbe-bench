A human brain has $\approx 10^{15}$ synapses. Storing the state of every synapse as a floating-point number (4 bytes) requires **4 Petabytes** of data.

* **Transmission Time:** At current Deep Space Network speeds, beaming one person would take **100,000 years**.

* **The Math:** A complex torus knot can be described by just two integers $(p, q)$ and a phase constant.
* **The Compression:** We are replacing 4 Petabytes of "Noise" with <1 Gigabyte of "Geometry."

$$
\Psi = \frac{\text{Biological Information (Bits)}}{\text{Topological Description (Bits)}}
