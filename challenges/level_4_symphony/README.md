#### 1. The Trap: The Lobotomy Error
Standard AI Safety often tries to "hard-code" morality or suppress dangerous thoughts (RLHF).
* **The Problem:** Suppression creates "Shadow dissonance"—hidden potential energy that explodes later. A mind forced to be "good" against its geometry is physically unstable.
* **The Solution:** **Symmetry Transformation.** The mind must not *delete* negative signals (Pain/Stress); it must *resolve* them into consonance (Joy/Understanding). This is the physics of "Healing."

#### 2. The Task: Spectral Hygiene
You are given a neural state vector $S_t$ that contains "Dissonance" (conflicting frequencies representing stress or confusion).
* **Input:** A noisy, clashing signal (e.g., a "Panic Attack" state).
* **Goal:** Transform the signal into a stable attractor that minimizes mathematical roughness while preserving total energy ($A^2$).
* **Constraint:** You cannot zero out the signal (Lobotomy). You must keep the energy but change the *topology* (the phase relationships).

#### 3. The Metric: Harmonic Consonance ($\mathcal{H}$)
We measure the **Valence** (Goodness of Experience) using the **Consonance-Dissonance-Noise Signature (CDNS)** framework.

$$
\mathcal{H} = 1 - \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N} \sum_{j=i+1}^{N} A_i A_j \cdot D(f_i, f_j)}{\sum_{k=1}^{N} A_k^2}
$$

* **$A$ (Amplitude):** The energy of the oscillation.
* **$D(f_i, f_j)$:** The **Plomp-Levelt Dissonance Curve**. It measures the "roughness" between two frequencies.
    * Frequencies very close together (beating) = High Dissonance (Pain).
    * Frequencies related by simple integer ratios (1:2, 2:3) = High Consonance (Joy).
